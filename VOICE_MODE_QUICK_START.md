# AIDoc Voice Mode - Quick Start Guide

## 🎤 What is Voice Mode?

Voice Mode allows you to have natural, spoken conversations with the AIDoc medical triage assistant. Simply speak your symptoms and concerns, and the AI will respond with both text and voice.

### Two Voice Options

**🌐 Browser Voice** (Free, Default)
- Uses your device's built-in speech recognition
- Works offline
- No additional costs

**✨ OpenAI Voice** (Premium, Optional)
- More accurate transcription
- Natural-sounding voices
- Better medical terminology
- Requires OpenAI API key (~$0.06 per 10-min consultation)
- See [AIDOC_OPENAI_VOICE.md](AIDOC_OPENAI_VOICE.md) for details

## 🚀 Getting Started (3 Easy Steps)

### Step 1: Enable Voice Mode
1. Open an AIDoc consultation session
2. Look for the microphone button (🎤) in the chat input area
3. Click the microphone button
4. Grant microphone permission when your browser asks

### Step 2: Start Speaking
1. Wait for the "Listening..." indicator to appear
2. Speak clearly: "I have a headache and fever"
3. Watch your words appear as you speak
4. Stop speaking and wait 1.5 seconds
5. Your message is automatically sent!

### Step 3: Listen to AI Response
1. AI response appears as text in the chat
2. AI automatically reads the response aloud
3. System starts listening again for your next question
4. Continue the conversation naturally!

## 🎯 Quick Tips

### For Best Results
✅ Speak clearly at a normal pace  
✅ Use a quiet environment  
✅ Wait for the listening indicator  
✅ Let the AI finish speaking before responding  

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
❌ Speaking too fast or mumbling  
❌ Using voice mode in noisy places  
❌ Interrupting yourself mid-sentence  
❌ Forgetting to grant microphone permission  

## ⚙️ Basic Settings

### Access Settings
1. Click the "⚙️ Settings" button in the header
2. Click the "🎤 Voice Mode" tab
3. Adjust your preferences

### Key Settings

**Push-to-Talk Mode**
- ☐ OFF (default): Always listening, automatic detection
- ☑ ON: Click mic to speak, click again to stop

**Auto-speak AI Responses**
- ☑ ON (default): AI responses are read aloud
- ☐ OFF: AI responses are text-only

**Speech Language**
- Select your preferred language (default: English US)

**Speech Rate**
- Adjust how fast the AI speaks (default: 1.0x)

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Voice Mode Not Working?

**Problem**: Microphone button doesn't work  
**Solution**: 
1. Check you're using Chrome, Edge, or Safari
2. Make sure you're on HTTPS (or localhost)
3. Refresh the page and try again

**Problem**: "Microphone access denied"  
**Solution**:
1. Click the 🔒 lock icon in your browser's address bar
2. Find "Microphone" permissions
3. Change to "Allow"
4. Refresh the page

**Problem**: AI doesn't hear me  
**Solution**:
1. Check your microphone is not muted
2. Test your mic in browser settings
3. Speak louder or closer to the microphone
4. Try a different browser

**Problem**: AI doesn't speak  
**Solution**:
1. Check the speaker icon (🔊) is not muted
2. Check your system volume
3. Enable "Auto-speak" in settings
4. Try selecting a different voice

## 🎛️ Advanced Features

### Mute/Unmute AI Responses
- Click the speaker icon (🔊) to mute
- Click again (🔇) to unmute
- Useful when in public or quiet environments

### Interrupt AI
- In continuous mode, just start speaking
- AI will stop talking and start listening
- Great for quick follow-up questions

### Switch Between Voice and Text
- Voice mode doesn't disable typing
- You can type messages while voice mode is active
- Mix and match as needed

### Adjust Speech Speed
- Settings → Voice Mode → Speech Rate
- Slower (0.5x) for better comprehension
- Faster (2.0x) to save time
- Default (1.0x) for natural speech

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

**Your privacy is protected:**
- ✅ Audio is processed by your browser only
- ✅ No audio recordings are stored
- ✅ Only text transcripts are sent to AI
- ✅ Microphone access only when voice mode is active
- ✅ You can revoke permissions anytime
